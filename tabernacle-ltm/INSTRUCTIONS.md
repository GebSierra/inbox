## How this town differs from James and Colossae
- It follows the book *The Chapter Code* (https://a.co/d/04W9GCw3), not the five-verses-per-room method: one stop per chapter of Hebrews, one picture per stop. It is a companion to the book, and says so on every stop; the owner's photos from the book are not in the repo.
- `town.json` has no `bookFile` and `"built": 0`, so the engine builds no rooms. `world/tour.js` pushes thirteen plain stops (no loci) into `STATIONS`; each carries its chapter, place, picture and theme (`st.hb`) for the caption card.
- `world/hebrews.js` builds the pictures (after `camp.js`, so the camp's random layout is unchanged). Hebrews 6, 7 and 9 also change `world/tabernacle.js`: the lampstand's branches fall away, the table has 7 loaves (Abraham holds the other 5), the veil is torn in two.
- The engine makes the current stop the one whose point is nearest the spot 5.5 m ahead of you, so no two stops may share a standing place and facing (Hebrews 10–12 look at the ark from three places).
- `world/after.js` runs after `engine/finish.js`: the caption card, the pillar of cloud, altar smoke, incense, the bird's-eye cutaway (the coverings in `ROOF` hide), and the HUD shows area names instead of "Chapter n".
- Measurements: `world/tabernacle.js`, in cubits of 18 in (Exodus 25–27, 30, 36–38); the laver (no size in Exodus) is drawn wide and low for Hebrews 4. Camp: `world/camp.js` (Numbers 2–3).
- The middle bays of the gate and the door are drawn back so you can walk through; the veil's middle is torn.
