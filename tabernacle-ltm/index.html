Based on history
Built to the measurements in Exodus 25–27, 30 and 36–38, with a cubit of 18 inches: a courtyard of 100 × 50 cubits, a tent of 30 × 10 × 10, the most holy place a 10-cubit cube in the middle of the western half, the altar in the middle of the eastern half.
The camp round it follows Numbers 2–3: the Levites close in, Moses and Aaron at the door, the four camps under their standards.
Exodus does not say everything: the shape of the cherubim, the colours of the tachash skins, a ridge on the roof, the colour of each tribe’s standard and the figures woven into the curtains are imagined. The middle of the gate, the door and the veil are drawn back so you can walk through.
