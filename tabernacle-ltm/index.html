A companion to The Chapter Code
The thirteen pictures here, one for each chapter of Hebrews at its place in the tabernacle, come from the book The Chapter Code, which teaches the outline of Hebrews with a tabernacle memory palace. This walk-through only supplements it: to understand and remember the full outline of Hebrews, get the book.
The most holy place (Hebrews 10–12) is left as it is: picture those three yourself.

Based on history
Built to the measurements in Exodus 25–27, 30 and 36–38, with a cubit of 18 inches: a courtyard of 100 × 50 cubits, a tent of 30 × 10 × 10, the most holy place a 10-cubit cube in the middle of the western half, the altar in the middle of the eastern half.
The camp round it follows Numbers 2–3: the Levites close in, Moses and Aaron at the door, the four camps under their standards.
Exodus does not say everything: the shape of the cherubim, the colours of the tachash skins, a ridge on the roof, the colour of each tribe’s standard and the figures woven into the curtains are imagined. The middle of the gate and the door are drawn back so you can walk through; the veil is torn in two (Hebrews 9). The laver is drawn large enough for Hebrews 4.
