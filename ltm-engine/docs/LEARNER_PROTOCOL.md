# Learner recall test: James 1:1–10

A short protocol to check whether the palace actually helps someone remember. It takes about 40 minutes on day 1 and 10 minutes on day 8.

## Before you start
- The learner hasn't memorized James 1 before.
- Print or open the ESV text of James 1:1–10.
- The James palace is built: chapter 1, rooms 1–2 (verses 1–5 and 6–10).

## Day 1
1. **Rote (10 min).** The learner reads verses 1–10 aloud, each verse 10–20 times, until they can say each one roughly.
2. **Place (15 min).** They walk room 1, then room 2, in the palace. At each object (near-left, far-left, far-right, near-right, centre) they make their own vivid picture for that verse and say the verse aloud.
3. **Distraction (5 min).** Any unrelated task, such as a word puzzle.
4. **Recall test A (5 min).** With the screen off and no text, they recite verses 1–10 in order. Record it on a phone.
5. **Score A.** For each verse:
   - **2:** verbatim or one small slip
   - **1:** the meaning is right but the wording is off, or part is missing
   - **0:** missed
   - Also record any **order errors** (a verse recited in the wrong place).

   The maximum is 20.

## Day 8 (one week later, no review in between)
6. **Recall test B (5 min).** Same as test A, with no warning and no text.
7. **Score B** the same way.

## Optional comparison (better evidence)
- On day 1, have the same learner also memorize **James 2:1–10 by rote only** (the same 10 minutes of reading, no palace).
- Test both passages together on day 1 and day 8.
- The palace is helping if James 1 scores higher and keeps more on day 8 than James 2.

## Record
| Learner | Day-1 score (/20) | Day-1 order errors | Day-8 score (/20) | Day-8 order errors | Notes (which images worked) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Success threshold (suggested):** at least 16/20 on day 1 and at least 12/20 on day 8, with no more than one order error. Treat this as a pilot. One learner isn't proof, but five learners with consistent results is meaningful evidence.
