About this palace
The verses, their topics and their order come from Robert J. Morgan’s book 100 Bible Verses Everyone Should Know by Heart (B&H Publishing, 2010). The book explains why memorizing Scripture matters, gives tips for learning by heart, and has notes on every verse. If this palace helps you, get the book to learn more.
This palace is a companion to the book: one road through Bible lands, one district per topic and one room per passage. Some passages run a few verses longer than the book’s reference so each thought is complete. The places are simplified or imagined so each room is easy to remember.
Tap a room’s sign to read the passage in the BSB or the ESV.
