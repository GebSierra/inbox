## A. Why it feels random now (what I saw from above and at the stops)
- The **Gate Stoa** is a loose colonnade standing across the middle of the court inside the gate; its five objects stand on open paving.
- The square's edges are **broken**: stoa fragments, then a gap of dirt and palms, then the Tower of the Winds and the Tholos poking out of the edge, then more gaps.
- At every stop the objects stand **in front of** a building on open paving, not inside a room.
- The houses round the agora are a random scatter, so there are no real streets.

## B. The agora fix (chapter 1, same six rooms, same order, same objects)
1. **One closed ring of stoas** round the square, with no gaps. Each room is a roofed **bay** in that ring, with back and side walls.
2. **Objects stand inside the bay**, under the roof. You stand at the bay's mouth on the square, looking in.
3. **1 Gate Stoa** becomes the enclosed court between the two gate towers and a new inner arch: walls on both sides, the objects inside it, you standing in the outer archway. The freestanding colonnade goes.
4. Left side going up: **2 Tower of the Winds**, **3 Tholos**, each set into the west stoa line with its own bay. Right side coming back: **4 Lamp Stoa**, **5 Mirror Court** (exedra), **6 Almshouse Portico**. Clockwise order is unchanged.
5. The dirt, stray palms and gaps behind the stoas become **house blocks on a straight street grid**, the way Greek cities like Miletus and Priene were laid out, so streets read as streets.
6. The altar stays at the centre of the square.

## C. The town route (chapters 2–5, districts already approved)
Gate → Agora (1) → out of the agora's south passage onto a **colonnaded avenue** → **Synagogue** (2), whose forecourt door opens off the north end of the avenue on the east side, as at Sardis → back out and down the avenue: **Craftsmen's Street** (3), shops on both sides → the avenue ends at the quay: **Harbour warehouses** (4), a horrea courtyard building beside the pier → out through a **new west postern gate** along the shore → **Farm & vineyard** (5) in the walled orchard outside the west wall.
