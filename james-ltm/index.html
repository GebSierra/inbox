Based on history
The agora: the Athenian Agora (stoas round an open square, tholos, altar at the centre).
The synagogue: first-century Galilean and Judean synagogues (inward-facing stone benches, columns, flat roof, ritual baths), with Sardis.
The harbour warehouses: the horrea of Ostia.
The craftsmen’s street: the shops of Sardis.
The vineyard: farm watchtowers and rock-cut winepresses (Isaiah 5:2).
Some details are simplified or imagined so each room is easy to remember.
